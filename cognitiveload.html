Project Overview

This is an hackathon project conducted by data science department of our univeristy. Our proposed solution is to have an open evaluation system for semester examinations.

We also proposed to have an user friendly website for our university with an option to choose our electives. This user frindly website will be benifitial for both students and staffs.

Research Motivation

We faced the problem while choosing our electives because there is different timings for different electives and we have to go to different departments to ask about the electives and sometimes our department timings clashes with the elective timings, so we decided to keep an centralised system in our new website to choose electives with time and syllabus.

Objectives

  • End to end automated website management system
  • Smooth elective enroolment
  • Open evaluation system

Methodology

We had asked suggestions from students of our university, office people and with thier inputs we had designed the website in VS-code
