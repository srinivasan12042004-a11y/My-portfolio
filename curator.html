Curator Booking system

3rd Hackathon project

2024

Project Overview

This is our 3rd hackathon project where we made a website for booking care takers for elderdy and disabled people

Usually elderly and disabled people has to rely on others when they want to go to any place. Our website can solve the dependency problem for elderly people they can simply book the care taker through the app for their particular time

Research Motivation

To overcome the dependency problem for elderly and disabled people

Objectives

  • Registered users can book their own care takers at particular time and charges will be hourly based
  • Care takers also can register by providing their complete details and their aadhar will be verifed

Methodology

We made the website using VS code and for validate it we had took survey among the disabled students from our college and some elderly people with the college surroundings
