Sense of Agency using EEG

Cognitive Science

2026

Project Overview

I was involved in designing the paradigms for sense of agency. I designed 3 paradigms, it was replication from the previous studies.

This also involves EEG+tACS the main aim of this paradigm is to find whether high density tACS affects in processing of self vs non-self

Research Motivation

I choose this project because of my interest in sense of agency and i also want to learn EEG

Objectives

  • Develop paradigms for sense of agency according to our population
  • To check whether 60 Hz tACS will improve SoA processing in tempo-parietl junction

Methodology

We had deployed these paradigms in Psychopy, Participants will undergo a screening task and trait questionaree and after that participant will perform the task at 3 time points, one at 60 Hz tACS next at 10 Hz tACS and SHAM tACS for baseline comparison
